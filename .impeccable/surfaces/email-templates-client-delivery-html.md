## THESIS

A premium client handoff that makes contracts, access details, and next actions feel deliberate and important. The email should read like a prepared delivery package from a serious software company, not a transactional system notice.

## OWN-WORLD

Traffodata's email world uses a deep black canvas, cream light, indigo signal, luminous technical atmosphere, and centered editorial pacing. The visual language takes its spatial confidence from the supplied reference while using original Traffodata imagery, identity, and operational content.

## STORY

The recipient enters through a branded live-text hero, receives a concise handoff note, verifies delivery metadata, opens contract and software access modules, collects resources, understands the next steps, and leaves with a clear support path.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The first viewport shows the Traffodata mark, project name, and large editable delivery title over a cream-to-indigo raster hero, followed by a concise client greeting and the primary action.

## FORM

A 600px table-based email with an original 1200px raster hero, live HTML text overlay, Outlook VML fallback, cream typography, indigo gradient actions, restrained rounded panels, and mobile-stacked detail rows. Direction shaped from the approved supplied reference; seed key: supplied editorial invitation.

## FINISH

The surface brief is the email system record; a repository-wide DESIGN.md is not required for this isolated surface. Every shipping raster carries provenance, and production asset URLs must return successfully before a client send.
